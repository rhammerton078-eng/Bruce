# Exploring Mars

Mars is the fourth planet from the Sun and is often called the Red Planet because iron oxide (rust) covers its surface. Mars has two small moons, Phobos and Deimos.

Olympus Mons on Mars is the tallest volcano in the Solar System, about three times the height of Mount Everest.

NASA's Perseverance rover landed in Jezero Crater in February 2021. It searches for signs of ancient microbial life and carried Ingenuity, the first helicopter to fly on another planet. Earlier, the Curiosity rover (2012) found evidence that Gale Crater once held a lake.

Like the Moon, Mars was probably shaped by giant impacts early in Solar System history.
