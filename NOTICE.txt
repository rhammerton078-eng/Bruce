ATLAS · Mnemo AI  (version 1.0.0)
Created by Robert Bruce Jr. C. Hammerton
BSIT, College of Computer Studies, Colegio de Santa Rita de San Carlos, Inc.

ATLAS is an offline AI that starts knowing nothing and learns from what you give it.
Mnemo's knowledge (your files, facts and the idea map) is stored on your own computer.
